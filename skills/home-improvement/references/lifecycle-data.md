# System lifespans (bundled fallback)

Typical expected service lives for common home systems, for when a search for
current figures isn't possible. These are broad national ranges compiled from
industry life-expectancy studies (e.g., NAHB/InterNACHI-style tables) and
manufacturer guidance; local climate, installation quality, and maintenance
move them a lot. When you use a value from this file, label it
**"bundled estimate — verify"** and prefer a searched figure or the user's
inspection report.

There are deliberately **no prices here**. Replacement and remodel costs vary
too much by region and year to bundle; always search for a current local
range (project type + city or ZIP + year) and cite it with an as-of date, or
mark the cost missing.

## How to use

- **Replace-by year** = install year + the low end of the range for a system
  in poor/fair condition, mid-range for good condition. Show the range, not
  just the year.
- **% of life used** (for [need-want.md](need-want.md)) = age ÷ midpoint of
  the range.
- A system with no known install year: estimate from the house's year built
  and any remodel history, label it "estimated," and suggest how to find the
  real date (data plate, permit history, inspection).
